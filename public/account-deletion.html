Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: August 15, 2026

You can delete your Engnova account and associated data at any time. Two options:

Option 1 — From inside the app

Open Engnova → Profile → Settings → Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm. Deletion completes immediately.

(If you don’t see this option, use the email path below — we’re rolling the in-app control out.)

Option 2 — By email

Email engnovaapp@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify the request and process it within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation once your data has been removed.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in information (email, phone if applicable, hashed password, provider identifiers).
  • Your learning data — progress, answers, scores, streaks, practice recordings.
  • Notification tokens and cached content.

What may be retained

We retain a limited record of completed payments where required for accounting or legal reasons (typically 5 years under Uzbekistan tax rules). These records contain the payment transaction details but not your active account or learning data, and are never used for any other purpose.

Third-party providers

When you delete your account, we instruct our service providers (Supabase, Resend, and others listed in the Privacy Policy) to delete their copies of your data on their normal schedules, typically within 30–90 days of our request.

Google & Telegram sign-in

Deleting your Engnova account does not revoke the Engnova app’s permission on the Google or Telegram side. To fully revoke:

  • Google: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Engnova.
  • Telegram: open Settings → Devices → Active sessions and terminate the Engnova bot session.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests complete within 30 days. Backups containing your data are overwritten in the normal course within 90 days.
