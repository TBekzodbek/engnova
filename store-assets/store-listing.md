### EN

Engnova — English from the ground up, and full IELTS preparation, in one app.

Engnova hosts two independent English products behind a single chooser. Open the app, pick your track, and start practicing. You can switch tracks any time — your progress in each stays waiting for you.

**CEFR Academy**
Structured English from A0 to C1, one lesson at a time.
- Daily lesson plan built around your current level
- Full mock exam covering reading, listening, writing and speaking
- Writing practice with AI feedback on structure, grammar and vocabulary
- Speaking practice with pronunciation feedback (microphone required)
- Progress tracked as CEFR levels — A1, A2, B1, B2, C1

**IELTS Level**
Full four-section preparation for the official IELTS exam.
- Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking practice
- Realistic mock tests in exam-like conditions
- AI band-score feedback on written and spoken responses
- Progress tracked as IELTS bands so you always know where you stand

**Why Engnova**
- Two full products in one app — no need to juggle separate apps for general English and IELTS
- Uzbekistan-first: Uzbek is the default language, and the interface is written for local learners
- Honest progress signals — CEFR levels and IELTS bands, not vague streak gimmicks
- No ads, no third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs

**Languages**
Uzbek (default), Russian, English. Switch inside the app any time.

**Requirements**
- Android 8.0 or newer
- Microphone permission is required only for speaking practice — recordings stay on your device
- Internet connection required: lesson content is served from our website

**A note on payments**
Payments happen on our website, not inside the app. All plans are billed by our partners paycom.uz and click.uz — Uzbekistan's national payment gateways. When you tap a paid plan, the app opens a secure browser window to complete the purchase, then returns you to the app.
