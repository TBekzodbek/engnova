# Engnova — Closed Testing Recruitment Kit

Google Play requires personal developer accounts to run a **closed test with at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days** before an app can be promoted to production. This file has everything needed to recruit and follow up with those 12 people.

Package: `uz.engnova.app` · Support email: `engnovaapp@gmail.com`

---

## 1. What owner needs to do first (setup, once)

Do this once, before sending any recruitment message.

1. **Create a Google Group for testers** (recommended)
   - Go to https://groups.google.com and create a new group, e.g. `engnova-testers@googlegroups.com`.
   - Privacy: set "Who can join the group" = **Anyone can ask** (or **Invited users only** if you want tighter control).
   - Play Console accepts a Google Group as the tester list — much easier than pasting 12 addresses. Adding or removing testers later is just adding/removing group members.
2. **OR use a plain email list** (fallback)
   - In Play Console → **Testing → Closed testing → Testers**, paste 12 Gmail addresses (one per line). Each tester must sign in to the Play Store with that exact Google account.
3. **Enroll yourself first**
   - Add your own Gmail to the group / list.
   - In Play Console open the **opt-in URL** (Testing → Closed testing → **Copy link**).
   - Accept the invite in your Google account, then install from Play Store on your device. Confirm the app launches, chooser appears, and both tracks (cefracademy.uz, ieltslevel.uz) load. If your own install works, the flow is clean.
4. **Set the closed-testing track region**
   - Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → **Countries / regions**. For UZ recruits, set to **Uzbekistan** at minimum. Add other countries only if you plan to recruit outside UZ.
5. **Keep the tester count at 12+ for the full 14 days**
   - Play counts **opted-in testers**, not installers. Do not let anyone opt out during the window — that resets the clock.
   - Recruit 15 to be safe; a couple will always ghost.
6. **Save the opt-in URL somewhere durable**
   - The link looks like `https://play.google.com/apps/testing/uz.engnova.app`. Paste it into every message below in place of `[PLAY_TESTER_URL]`.

## 5. FAQ — things testers will ask

### Q: How do I install it?

1. Tap the link you got (`[PLAY_TESTER_URL]`) on your Android phone.
2. A page opens saying you were invited to test **Engnova**. Tap **Become a tester**.
3. Tap **Download it on Google Play** — Play Store opens with the app page.
4. Tap **Install**.

If Play Store says "Item not found" the first time, wait 10-15 minutes and reopen the link. Google needs time to index the invite.

### Q: Is this the final version?

No — this is a testing build. Small bugs are possible. If anything looks broken, please reply to the message you got or write to `engnovaapp@gmail.com` with:
- What you tapped
- What happened (a screenshot helps a lot)
- Phone model + Android version if you know it

### Q: Does it cost money to install?

No. The app is free to install and free to open. Any paid subscriptions happen on the website (cefracademy.uz / ieltslevel.uz), not inside the app itself.

### Q: Can I keep it after testing ends?

Yes. When the 14-day test wraps and the app is published to Play Store officially, your installed copy will auto-update to the live version. You can also uninstall any time — there is no lock-in.

### Q: Will you see anything on my phone?

No. The app only stores what you'd expect for an English-learning app (your login, your progress on the website). It doesn't read contacts, photos, or messages. The permissions the app requests are:
- **Internet** — to load the website
- **Microphone** — only if you use speaking practice inside the website

### Q: I signed up but the Play Store link still doesn't open — what now?

- Make sure the Google account signed in to Play Store on your phone is the same one that accepted the tester invite.
- Wait 15 minutes after accepting, then retry.
- If it still fails, write to `engnovaapp@gmail.com` with the Gmail you used — the account will be added directly.
